TSA HUB | Self-Employed

  • TSA HUB is an independently developed, student-focused application designed to centralize Technology Student Association (TSA) information and streamline competition discovery and preparation. It consolidates publicly available content from official TSA websites, PDF documents, competition guidelines, and organizational resources into a unified, accessible platform for middle and high school students.

Minecraft Plugin

SyncEquip

  • SyncEquip is a Minecraft Java plugin designed to make coordinated equipment sequences easier to pull off, especially when working with large groups of players. Imagine an entire squad putting on their armor, drawing their weapons, and raising their shields at exactly the same time. Coordinating moments like these manually can be difficult, particularly when dozens of players need to act in perfect sync.
  • SyncEquip handles that synchronization automatically, allowing entire squads to equip and unequip their gear together with customizable timing and equipment loadouts. Players can join squads through invitations or simply by stepping on a designated block, making it easier to organize large groups without assigning everyone individually.

Minecraft Plugin

PortalDirector

  • PortalDirector is a Minecraft Java plugin designed to simplify the creation and coordination of large-scale cinematic scenes, especially for content creators working with dozens or even hundreds of players. Imagine an entire sky filled with Nether portals appearing one after another, armies emerging from different directions, or waves of TNT minecarts launching across the battlefield. Creating scenes like these manually can require extensive preparation, precise timing, and coordination between multiple players.
  • PortalDirector brings these mechanics together into a single system, allowing creators to generate customizable portal formations, manage player appearances, and coordinate TNT attacks. It also introduces automated cinematic sequences that handle multiple actions through a single command, making complex scenes easier to organize and execute without manually controlling every individual element.

Hey Charlotte | FBLA 2026-2027 Competition

  • Hey Charlotte is a centralized city information platform developed for the 2026–2027 FBLA competition, designed to bring together essential Charlotte resources that are currently distributed across numerous city, community, school, tourism, and local websites.
  • The platform is built for students, newcomers, residents, and visitors, providing a more direct way to access information about attractions, events, transportation, education, local services, opportunities, dining, entertainment, and other everyday resources. Rather than focusing on a single audience or use case, Hey Charlotte is designed as a broader digital entry point for understanding and navigating the city.
  • The project’s long-term objective is to grow beyond the competition into a widely used Charlotte resource, potentially through collaboration with an established local organization or existing city-focused platform. One possible direction would be adapting the platform for an organization that already operates a major Charlotte-facing domain, such as Charlotte’s Got a Lot, allowing the project to evolve from an independent website into a more broadly adopted public resource.
