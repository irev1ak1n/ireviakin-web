Chrome Extension

LinkWise

  • LinkWise is an AI-powered LinkedIn Chrome extension engineered to streamline professional discovery, opportunity evaluation, and career decision-making. It interprets profiles and job listings against user-defined criteria, producing contextual match scores, surfacing high-value evidence, and identifying the most consequential information within the page.
  • Its current capabilities include intelligent profile analysis, job assessment, profile optimization, advanced filtering, and AI-assisted recommendations designed to reduce friction across professional research and job-search workflows.
  • LinkWise is actively evolving into a broader professional productivity ecosystem, with planned functionality spanning resume development, personal branding, content generation, workflow automation, and career management. The long-term objective is to establish a scalable commercial AI platform for professional growth and LinkedIn productivity.

Mobile App

TSA HUB

  • TSA HUB is an independently developed, student-focused application designed to centralize Technology Student Association (TSA) information and streamline competition discovery and preparation. It consolidates publicly available content from official TSA websites, PDF documents, competition guidelines, and organizational resources into a unified, accessible platform for middle and high school students.
  • Its core capabilities include a comprehensive competitive event directory, personalized event recommendations, competition requirements, annual themes, official resources, and state-specific information. The platform also integrates an interactive calendar, customizable reminders, and intuitive search tools to improve accessibility and student organization.
  • TSA HUB's long-term objective is to become a nationwide platform for TSA members, simplifying access to essential resources, improving student engagement, and making competition discovery and preparation more efficient across all chapters and states.
